# Appendix B — Printable Checklists
# Додаток B — Контрольні листи для друку

**🇨🇦 EN** — Print these, laminate them if you can, and keep them aboard. Checklists exist because memory fails under stress and time pressure — that's not a weakness, it's how brains work. Professional mariners and pilots use checklists for exactly this reason.

**🇺🇦 UA** — Розпечатайте це, заламінуйте, якщо можете, і тримайте на борту. Контрольні листи існують, бо пам'ять підводить під стресом і в спішці — це не слабкість, це те, як працює мозок. Професійні моряки й пілоти використовують чек-листи саме тому.

---

## B.1 Pre-departure — every trip / Перед виходом — кожен раз

### Shore-side / На берегі
- ☐ **Marine forecast** checked — wind, gusts, **visibility**, sea state, warnings
- ☐ **Tide** checked — for departure **and return** time
- ☐ **Go / no-go decision** made honestly (see [Module 09 §9.7](../modules/09-weather-halifax.md))
- ☐ **Sail plan filed** — someone ashore knows where, who, when back, and **what to do if you're not**
- ☐ Phone charged, in a waterproof case
- ☐ Warm layers and foul-weather gear aboard, for everyone
- ☐ Water and food aboard
- ☐ Sunscreen, hat, sunglasses
- ☐ Seasickness medication taken by anyone who needs it — **an hour before departure**

### Boat / Судно
- ☐ **Bilge** checked — dry, or pumped
- ☐ **Seacocks** in correct positions
- ☐ **Rigging inspected** — shrouds, stays, turnbuckles, pins, chainplates, gooseneck (see [Module 03 §3.3](../modules/03-boat-anatomy.md))
- ☐ **Sails rigged** — halyards attached, battens in, outhaul rigged
- ☐ **Sheets led correctly**, outside the shrouds, with **stopper knots in the ends**
- ☐ Topping lift holding the boom
- ☐ **Fuel** level adequate for the trip **plus a reserve**
- ☐ **Battery** on, voltage adequate
- ☐ **Engine: WOBBLE** — Water, Oil, Belts, Bolts, Leaks, Electrics
- ☐ **Raw-water seacock OPEN**
- ☐ **Nothing in the water astern** — no lines, no dinghy painter
- ☐ Fenders and docklines ready, departure plan agreed
- ☐ Winch handles aboard and stowed

### Safety equipment located and functional / Спорядження безпеки
- ☐ **PFDs** — one per person, correct size, accessible, **and being worn**
- ☐ Harnesses and tethers, if conditions warrant
- ☐ **Flares** — present and **in date**
- ☐ **Fire extinguisher(s)** — present, charged, accessible
- ☐ **First aid kit**
- ☐ **VHF** — working, on Ch 16, and a radio check done
- ☐ Handheld VHF charged, in the grab bag
- ☐ **Bilge pump and its handle**
- ☐ Buoyant heaving line
- ☐ Lifebuoy / horseshoe with light
- ☐ Boarding ladder or reboarding device
- ☐ Sound-signalling device (horn/whistle)
- ☐ Navigation lights tested
- ☐ Anchor and rode ready to deploy
- ☐ Charts, tide tables, hand-bearing compass aboard
- ☐ Knife accessible from the cockpit
- ☐ Radar reflector up, if fog is possible

### After engine start / Після запуску двигуна
- ☐ **Cooling water flowing from the exhaust** — if not, **STOP IMMEDIATELY**
- ☐ Oil pressure and temperature normal
- ☐ Alternator charging
- ☐ Gear engages ahead and astern
- ☐ Steering responds

## B.2 Crew safety brief — every trip, no exceptions / Інструктаж екіпажу

**🇨🇦 EN** — Two minutes. **Point at each item physically** as you say it. Do this even with experienced crew.

- ☐ **"Here are the PFDs, here's how to wear one, here's the whistle and light."**
- ☐ **"Here's the fire extinguisher. The flares. The first aid kit."**
- ☐ **"Here's the VHF. This is channel 16. This is high power. Here's the MAYDAY card — read it now."**
- ☐ **"Here's the bilge pump and its handle."**
- ☐ **"Here's the engine start and the kill switch."**
- ☐ **"Here are the seacocks."** (On a boat you're skippering for the first time, and for overnight crew.)
- ☐ **"Watch the boom. When I say 'gybe-ho', get your head down and stay down."**
- ☐ **"One hand for the boat. Move on the high side. Nobody forward of the mast without telling me."**
- ☐ **"If someone goes over: shout, throw something that floats, and one of you points at them and does absolutely nothing else."**
- ☐ **"If *I* go over, here is exactly what you do: [stop the boat / throw flotation / press MOB on the plotter / MAYDAY on channel 16]."**
- ☐ **"We're going here, back around this time, and I've told someone ashore."**
- ☐ **"Questions? Anyone prone to seasickness? Anyone need medication now?"**

**🇺🇦 UA** — Дві хвилини. **Показуйте фізично на кожен предмет.** Пункт про «якщо *я* впаду за борт» — найважливіший і майже завжди пропускається.

## B.3 Man overboard — immediate actions / Людина за бортом

**🇨🇦 EN — First five seconds, simultaneously:**

1. ☐ **SHOUT "MAN OVERBOARD!"**
2. ☐ **THROW flotation** — immediately, even if it lands far away
3. ☐ **PRESS MOB** on the GPS/plotter
4. ☐ **ASSIGN A SPOTTER** — one person, points at them, does nothing else, ever
5. ☐ **DO NOT LOSE SIGHT OF THEM**

**Then:**
6. ☐ **Quick Stop:** head up, tack leaving the jib backed, come back
7. ☐ Approach **from downwind**, on a close reach, controlling speed by luffing
8. ☐ Stop with them **on the leeward side, just forward of amidships**
9. ☐ **Engine in NEUTRAL** before they're near the stern
10. ☐ **Get a line on them and secure them to the boat** — before attempting to lift
11. ☐ Lift with a **halyard and winch** if needed. Keep them **horizontal**.
12. ☐ Out of the wind, out of wet clothes, into dry insulation. **Warm gradually.**
13. ☐ **PAN PAN or MAYDAY.** Get medical help — cold-water immersion has delayed effects.

**🇺🇦 UA — Перші п'ять секунд, одночасно:** КРИЧІТЬ · КИНЬТЕ плавуче · НАТИСНІТЬ MOB · ПРИЗНАЧТЕ СПОСТЕРІГАЧА (лише показує, більше нічого) · НЕ ВТРАЧАЙТЕ З ВИДУ.

## B.4 Reefing — when and how / Взяття рифів

### Reef NOW if / Беріть риф ЗАРАЗ, якщо:
- ☐ Heel is consistently over **20–25°**
- ☐ Heavy weather helm — you're fighting the tiller
- ☐ The leeward rail is going under
- ☐ You're constantly spilling wind to keep control
- ☐ The crew look worried, or have gone quiet
- ☐ **You are asking yourself whether to reef.** ← This one alone is sufficient.

### Procedure / Процедура
1. ☐ **Reduce load** — head up, or better, **heave-to**
2. ☐ **Take up the topping lift**
3. ☐ **Ease the main halyard** to the reef mark
4. ☐ **Secure the new tack** — cringle on the horn, or tension the luff line
5. ☐ **Haul the reefing line at the clew** tight along the boom
6. ☐ **Re-tension the halyard**
7. ☐ Tidy loose sail with reef ties — **do not trap the foot**
8. ☐ **Ease the topping lift**, sheet in, re-trim
9. ☐ **Reduce the headsail too** — or you'll be unbalanced with lee helm

## B.5 Docking / Швартування

### Before the approach / До підходу
- ☐ Decided **exactly** where you're going and which side is to the dock
- ☐ **Wind and current** direction and strength assessed
- ☐ Approaching **into** the stronger of the two
- ☐ **Crew briefed:** who steps off, which line first, to which cleat, who does what
- ☐ **Fenders out**, correct side, correct height
- ☐ **Docklines rigged**, led outside the lifelines, boat end secured, coiled ready
- ☐ **Abort plan agreed and said out loud:** *"If it looks wrong I'll say 'aborting', power out, and we'll go round."*

### The approach / Підхід
- ☐ **Slow.** Never faster than you're willing to hit the dock.
- ☐ Shallow angle, roughly 20–30°
- ☐ Out of gear and coasting for the last part
- ☐ Short burst of **reverse** to stop
- ☐ **One line on first**, turn taken on a cleat
- ☐ Adjust everything else at leisure

### Absolute rules / Абсолютні правила
- ☐ **Never** a hand, foot, or leg between boat and dock
- ☐ **Never** jump for the dock
- ☐ **Never** wrap a line around your hand
- ☐ **Abort freely.** It costs three minutes.

## B.6 Anchoring / Постановка на якір

- ☐ Anchorage chosen from the **chart**
- ☐ **Shelter from the FORECAST wind, for the whole time you'll be there**
- ☐ **Seabed type checked** — mud/sand good, rock/weed bad
- ☐ **Depth at low water** calculated, not estimated
- ☐ **Swinging room** checked at all states of tide — boats, rocks, shore, gear
- ☐ **Escape route** exists, usable in the dark and in the forecast conditions
- ☐ **Scope calculated:** (depth at **high** water + bow height) × **5 minimum, 7+ in wind**
- ☐ Approach **into wind/current**, stop over the spot
- ☐ **Lower under control** — don't dump the chain in a pile
- ☐ Pay out scope while drifting/motoring gently astern
- ☐ **Set it:** snub the rode, gentle reverse. Feel for juddering (dragging) vs solid (set)
- ☐ **Snubber rigged**
- ☐ **Transit taken** on two objects abeam
- ☐ Depth, position, and time noted
- ☐ **Anchor light / black ball** displayed
- ☐ Anchor alarm set
- ☐ Alarm set for the **tide turn**
- ☐ **Dragging plan** rehearsed verbally before bed

## B.7 Fog / Туман

- ☐ **PFDs on everyone.** Immediately.
- ☐ **Slow to a speed you can stop from** in the visibility you have
- ☐ **Sound fog signals** — sailing vessel: **one prolonged + two short**, at least every 2 min
- ☐ **Navigation lights on**
- ☐ **Dedicated lookout forward.** Stop the engine periodically to **listen**
- ☐ **Fix your position and log it.** Then log course and speed continuously for DR
- ☐ **Get out of the shipping channel** — into shallow water where big ships can't go
- ☐ **Radar reflector up**
- ☐ **Monitor VHF 16 and the VTS channels**
- ☐ Consider **anchoring** in shallow water clear of channels and simply waiting
- ☐ *There is no prize for arriving.*

---

## B.8 Fire / Пожежа

1. ☐ **Everyone into PFDs, on deck, upwind of the fire**
2. ☐ **Cut the fuel and power** — gas off at the bottle, fuel valve shut, batteries isolated
3. ☐ **Extinguisher at the base of the flames**, sweeping. Use a fire port rather than opening a compartment fully
4. ☐ **No water** on fuel or electrical fires
5. ☐ **MAYDAY early** — don't wait to see if you'll win. Cancel it if you do.
6. ☐ **Prepare to abandon** — grab bag, liferaft/dinghy, flares
7. ☐ **Do not re-enter** for possessions

---

## B.9 Taking on water / Надходження води

1. ☐ **Find the source** — usually a **seacock**, hose, stern gland, or cockpit drain, not a hull breach
2. ☐ **Close the seacock.** Wooden bungs are tied next to each one.
3. ☐ **Pump** — manual + electric + **bucket** (a bucket moves more water than most pumps)
4. ☐ **Slow the boat** — pressure into a hole rises sharply with speed
5. ☐ **MAYDAY while you still have power and a mast**
6. ☐ **Head for shallow water** if sinking is likely

---

## B.10 Grab bag contents / Вміст аварійної сумки

- ☐ Handheld VHF — waterproof, charged, ideally floating
- ☐ EPIRB or PLB
- ☐ Flares — parachute and hand-held
- ☐ Handheld GPS, or phone in a waterproof case + battery pack
- ☐ Drinking water
- ☐ High-energy food
- ☐ Space blankets / thermal protective aids
- ☐ First aid kit
- ☐ Knife
- ☐ Whistle and signalling mirror
- ☐ Waterproof torch + spare batteries
- ☐ Sunscreen and hat
- ☐ Copies of documents, and cash
- ☐ Essential prescription medication
- ☐ Pump or bailer for the raft/dinghy

## B.11 MAYDAY card — copy this next to your radio / Картка MAYDAY

```
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  VHF CHANNEL 16 — HIGH POWER
  (If DSC fitted: LIFT COVER, HOLD DISTRESS BUTTON first)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  "MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY"

  "THIS IS  ______________  ______________  ______________"
              (boat name, three times)

  "MAYDAY  ______________"
              (boat name, once)

  "MY POSITION IS  ________________________________"
              (lat/long, OR bearing + distance from a landmark)

  "________________________________________________"
              (nature of distress)

  "WE REQUIRE IMMEDIATE ASSISTANCE"

  "________  PERSONS ON BOARD"

  "________________________________________________"
              (boat description, colour, other information)

  "OVER"

  → RELEASE THE BUTTON AND LISTEN.
  → If no reply in 30 seconds, repeat the whole call.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  PAN PAN  = urgency, not life-threatening
  SÉCURITÉ = safety information
  MAYDAY RELAY = passing on someone else's distress
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  POSITION IS THE MOST IMPORTANT INFORMATION.
  If you can say only one thing, say WHERE YOU ARE.
  NEVER hesitate to call. There is no penalty for
  a good-faith MAYDAY.
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## B.12 End of day / Кінець дня

- ☐ Sails furled, flaked, and **covered**
- ☐ Halyards secured so they can't fly or slap
- ☐ Boom secured with the topping lift
- ☐ All lines coiled and stowed in their homes
- ☐ **Nothing left dangling in the water**
- ☐ Docklines set with **springs**, allowing for tide
- ☐ Fenders positioned at the actual contact points
- ☐ **Bilge dry**
- ☐ **Seacocks closed** as appropriate for the boat
- ☐ **Gas off at the bottle**
- ☐ Battery isolator / electrics as appropriate
- ☐ Engine seacock closed if that's the boat's practice
- ☐ Hatches shut, boat locked
- ☐ **Gear removed** — wet clothing, food, rubbish
- ☐ **Log filled in** (see [logbook](logbook-and-drills.md))
- ☐ **Told your shore contact you're back safely** ← the step everyone forgets, and it triggers false alarms when skipped
