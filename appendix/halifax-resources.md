## A.1 Governing bodies / Керівні органи

| Organisation | Role | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| **Sail Canada** | National governing body. Publishes the official keelboat/cruising standards, the CANSail dinghy program, and the ROC(M) and PCOC information pages. **The authoritative source for what each certification requires.** | sailing.ca |
| **Sail Nova Scotia** | Provincial governing body. Coordinates training and lists member clubs and programs across the province, including adult learn-to-sail options. **Start here to find courses.** | sailnovascotia.ca |
| **Transport Canada** | Federal regulator. Publishes the ***Safe Boating Guide* (TP 511)** — the authority on required safety equipment and boating law — plus the PCOC accredited-provider list and the Pleasure Craft Licence application. | tc.canada.ca |
| **CPS‑ECP** (Canadian Power and Sail Squadrons) | Delivers and examines the **ROC(M)** marine radio certificate on behalf of ISED Canada, and runs boating and navigation courses. | Tel. **1‑888‑277‑2628** |
| **Canadian Hydrographic Service** (Fisheries and Oceans Canada) | Charts, *Sailing Directions*, and tide tables. | charts.gc.ca |
| **Canadian Coast Guard / MCTS Halifax** | Marine safety, distress response, Continuous Marine Broadcast, and vessel traffic services. | VHF Ch 16 |
| **Halifax Port Authority** | Authority over all vessels in the port, commercial and recreational. Publishes the **Marine Safety Handbook** and **Port Information Guide** — worth reading if you sail in the harbour. | porthalifax.ca |
| **Boating Safety Infoline** (Transport Canada) | Ask for the list of accredited PCOC providers, or any boating safety question. | **1‑800‑267‑6687** |

## A.2 Yacht clubs and sailing schools / Яхт-клуби та школи

**🇨🇦 EN** — These are the places that teach adults in the Halifax area. Contact several — availability varies, and late-season cancellations are common.

### Armdale Yacht Club (AYC) / AYC Sailing School
- **Where:** Melville Island, in the **Northwest Arm**. A century-old club in a very sheltered location, which is what makes it good for beginners.
- **Programs:** An **Adult & Family Keelboating** program aimed at introducing people to sailing, with the option to be **evaluated and certified under Sail Canada's Start Keelboating** standard (participants 16+). Youth programs from about age 11. Classroom sessions run in winter as well as on-water sessions through summer and fall.
- **Membership:** Reportedly **not required** to take part, though **members receive a discount** on program fees. Confirm current arrangements.
- **Look for:** aycsailing.ca / aycss.ca, armdaleyachtclub.ns.ca
- **Why it's a good first call:** sheltered water, adult-focused program, no membership barrier, and a certification pathway.

### Royal Nova Scotia Yacht Squadron (RNSYS)
- **Where:** Purcell's Cove Road, on the **Northwest Arm**. One of the oldest yacht clubs in North America.
- **Programs:** **Adult programs** (adults only), taught by Sail Canada certified instructors, ranging from short introductory sessions to keelboat courses at basic and intermediate levels, plus learn-to-race coaching. Notably they run a short **"Try Sailing!"** introduction — a one-off session covering the basics on a cruise of the Northwest Arm. Separate youth programs.
- **Contact:** Main line reported as **(902) 477‑5653**; program enquiries to **sailnow@rnsys.com**
- **Look for:** rnsys.com/adultsailingprograms
- **Why it's a good first call:** the "Try Sailing" session is the ideal zero-commitment first step, and they teach both Basic and Intermediate keelboat levels.

### Dartmouth Yacht Club (DYC)
- **Where:** **Bedford Basin** area, Dartmouth side.
- **Programs:** Follows **CANSail and Sail Canada Keelboat** curricula. Classes for adults as well as youth (from about age 5), starting in summer and continuing into fall.
- **Racing:** Runs **Wednesday Night Racing** from **May into October**, in three series, on the Basin. Reported as welcoming guests, with **skippers regularly looking for crew** — the club's own guidance is essentially "meet us at the dock." Crew memberships exist and are cheaper than full membership.
- **Look for:** dartmouthyachtclub.ca
- **Why it matters to you:** **this is your best route to free hours on the water.** Wednesday night racing is the single highest-value thing on this page for a beginner.

### Bedford Basin Yacht Club
- **Where:** Bedford, at the head of the Basin.
- **Character:** Community club on sheltered water.
- **Worth contacting** about membership, moorings, and any adult programs.

### Nova Sail
- **Type:** Private sailing school.
- **Programs:** Courses based on the **Sail Canada or IYT** curriculum, with certification from introductory through to advanced levels. Principal instructor reported as Judy Robertson.
- **Look for:** novasail.ca
- **Why it matters:** private instruction can be more flexible on scheduling than club programs, and IYT is relevant if you plan to charter abroad.

### In Slocum's Wake Yacht Training
- **Type:** Private yacht training, Halifax.
- **Programs:** Accredited sailing courses with licensed instructors, offering sailing certifications.
- **Look for:** listed on nauticed.org and other school directories.

### Saint Mary's Boat Club
- **Where:** Northwest Arm.
- **Character:** Community/municipal boating facility on the Arm — worth investigating for accessible small-boat and paddling options.

### Beyond Halifax
- **St. Margaret's Bay area** — sailing clubs in the Hubbards and St. Margaret's areas.
- **Chester Yacht Club** — Mahone Bay, strong racing and cruising tradition.
- **Lunenburg Yacht Club** — Mahone Bay.
- **Cape Breton** — clubs on the Bras d'Or Lakes.

## A.3 What to ask when you call / Що питати при зверненні

**🇨🇦 EN** — A script, so you don't have to compose one. Adapt as needed.

> *"Hello — I'm a complete beginner, based in Halifax, and I'd like to learn to sail properly with the goal of getting Sail Canada certified. I have a few questions:*
>
> 1. *Do you have any spots or cancellations left in adult keelboat courses this season?*
> 2. *Can you put me on a waitlist, and when do you open registration for next spring?*
> 3. *Do you offer a short introductory or 'try sailing' session?*
> 4. *Which Sail Canada standards can I be certified in through you — Start Keelboating, Basic Cruising, Intermediate?*
> 5. *Do I need to be a member, and is there a crew or associate membership category?*
> 6. *What does it cost, all in?*
> 7. *Is there any way to crew on member boats to get hours — racing or cruising?*
> 8. *Do you run winter classroom courses — navigation, or the ROC(M) radio certificate?"*

**🇺🇦 UA** — Скрипт, щоб не складати самому:

> *«Добрий день — я абсолютний новачок з Галіфаксу і хочу навчитися вітрильному спорту як слід, з метою отримати сертифікацію Sail Canada. Кілька питань:*
>
> 1. *Чи є вільні місця або відмови на курсах кільових яхт для дорослих цього сезону?*
> 2. *Чи можна мене в список очікування, і коли відкривається реєстрація на весну?*
> 3. *Чи є короткий ознайомчий вихід?*
> 4. *Які стандарти Sail Canada я можу отримати через вас?*
> 5. *Чи потрібне членство, і чи є категорія crew/associate?*
> 6. *Скільки це коштує в цілому?*
> 7. *Чи є можливість ходити екіпажем на суднах членів, щоб набрати години — гонки або круїзи?*
> 8. *Чи проводите ви зимові аудиторні курси — навігація або ROC(M)?»*

## A.4 Weather, tides & charts / Погода, припливи та карти

| Resource | What for |
|---|---|
| **weather.gc.ca/marine** → *Halifax Harbour and Approaches* | **Your primary forecast.** Issued roughly 4×/day (about 0300, 1000, 1530, 2000). Wind, visibility, sea state, warnings. |
| **Continuous Marine Broadcast** — VHF **Ch 21B (WX8)** / **Ch 83B (WX9)** | The forecast on the water. Confirm which channel works locally. |
| **Weatheradio Canada** — VHF WX1–WX7 | Continuous forecasts, English and French. |
| **Canadian Hydrographic Service** — charts.gc.ca | Charts, ***Sailing Directions* for Nova Scotia** (essential for coastal cruising), and **tide tables**. |
| **Halifax Harbour chart** | Commonly **CHS 4237**; approaches commonly **CHS 4201**. **Verify current numbers and editions with a chart dealer.** |
| **Canadian Hurricane Centre** | Tropical storm tracks and warnings — check from mid-August. |
| **Windy / Windfinder / PredictWind** | Excellent for planning and pattern-spotting. **Not** a substitute for the official forecast and warnings. |
| **Navionics or equivalent** | Electronic charts on a phone/tablet. Useful; carry paper too. |
| **Halifax Harbour buoy observations** | Actual current conditions — better than a forecast for "should I go right now?" |

## A.5 Local practicalities / Місцева практика

**🇨🇦 EN**

**Season:** roughly **May (launch) to late October (haul-out)**. August and September are arguably the best sailing months — less fog than June/July, warmest water, reliable sea breeze.

**Water temperature:** roughly **8–12 °C in June**, peaking around **12–18 °C in July and August**. **Never warm enough to be safe unprotected.** See [Module 01](../modules/01-safety-and-cold-water.md).

**Fog:** over 100 days a year of fog or mist, **worst in June and July**.

**Tides:** semi-diurnal; range roughly **1.4 m mean, up to about 2 m on a large tide**. Modest, but current is significant in **the Narrows**.

**Prevailing summer wind:** **southwesterly sea breeze**, building through the afternoon, typically strongest 1400–1800.

**Hurricane season:** risk concentrated **August–October**.

**VHF:** Ch **16** distress and calling. Halifax vessel traffic commonly on **Ch 12 and Ch 14** — verify locally. CMB weather on **21B / 83B**.

**Chandleries and marine services:** Halifax has marine supply stores, riggers, sailmakers, surveyors, and boatyards. Ask at the yacht clubs for current recommendations — in a community this size, word of mouth is more reliable than online reviews.

**🇺🇦 UA**

**Сезон:** приблизно з **травня (спуск) до кінця жовтня (підйом)**. Серпень і вересень — імовірно найкращі місяці.

**Температура води:** приблизно **8–12 °C у червні**, максимум **12–18 °C у липні–серпні**. **Ніколи не досить тепла, щоб бути безпечною без захисту.**

**Туман:** понад 100 днів на рік, **найгірше в червні й липні**.

**Припливи:** напівдобові; амплітуда приблизно **1,4 м середня, до 2 м на великому припливі**. Помірна, але течія значна в **Narrows**.

**Переважний літній вітер:** **південно-західний морський бриз**, наростає протягом дня, найсильніший 14:00–18:00.

**Сезон ураганів:** риск сконцентрований **серпень–жовтень**.

**VHF:** канал **16** — біда і виклик. Рух суден Галіфаксу зазвичай на **каналах 12 і 14** — перевірте локально. Погода CMB на **21B / 83B**.

## A.7 Reading list / Список літератури

**🇨🇦 EN**

**Official and free — read these first:**
1. ***Safe Boating Guide* (TP 511)**, Transport Canada — **the single most important document.** Free. Authoritative on required equipment and Canadian boating law.
2. **Sail Canada's published standards** for each certification level — tells you exactly what you'll be examined on.
3. ***Sailing Directions* for Nova Scotia**, CHS — essential before any coastal cruise.
4. **Port of Halifax Marine Safety Handbook / Port Information Guide** — if you sail in the harbour.

**Books worth owning:**
1. ***The Annapolis Book of Seamanship*** — John Rousmaniere. The standard comprehensive reference. If you buy one book, buy this.
2. ***Sailing for Dummies*** or ***Start Sailing Right*** — genuinely good, genuinely simple beginner texts. No shame in either.
3. ***Chapman Piloting & Seamanship*** — the encyclopaedic North American reference.
4. ***Heavy Weather Sailing*** — Adlard Coles. For later, and sobering.
5. ***This Old Boat*** — Don Casey. If you buy a boat.
6. ***Storm Tactics Handbook*** — Lin and Larry Pardey. For the offshore stage.
7. Anything on the history of Nova Scotia schooners and the *Bluenose* — because you'll sail past that history, and it makes the place mean more.

**🇺🇦 UA**

**Офіційне і безкоштовне — читайте перш за все:**
1. ***Safe Boating Guide* (TP 511)**, Transport Canada — **найважливіший документ.** Безкоштовно.
2. **Опубліковані стандарти Sail Canada** для кожного рівня сертифікації.
3. ***Sailing Directions* для Нової Шотландії**, CHS — обов'язково перед будь-яким прибережним круїзом.
4. **Marine Safety Handbook порту Галіфакс.**

**Книги, які варто мати:** *The Annapolis Book of Seamanship* (Rousmaniere) — якщо купуєте одну книгу, купуйте цю · *Sailing for Dummies* / *Start Sailing Right* — справді добрі прості тексти для новачків · *Chapman Piloting & Seamanship* · *Heavy Weather Sailing* (Adlard Coles) — на потім · *This Old Boat* (Don Casey) — якщо купуєте судно · *Storm Tactics Handbook* (Pardey).

## A.8 Emergency contacts / Аварійні контакти

| Situation | Contact |
|---|---|
| **Marine distress — life-threatening** | **VHF Channel 16** (and press the **DSC distress button** if fitted). This reaches the Canadian Coast Guard **and every vessel in range.** |
| Marine distress — by phone | **911** (they will connect you to marine SAR) |
| Marine urgency (not life-threatening) | **PAN PAN on VHF Ch 16** |
| Vessel traffic / harbour movements | Halifax Traffic (MCTS), commonly **VHF Ch 12 / 14** — verify locally |
| Pollution report | Canadian Coast Guard |
| Boating safety questions | **1‑800‑267‑6687** (Transport Canada Boating Safety Infoline) |
| Weather on the water | **VHF Ch 21B / 83B** (Continuous Marine Broadcast) |

**🇨🇦 EN — Print this table and tape it inside the boat, next to the radio, along with your MAYDAY card.**

**🇺🇦 UA — Розпечатайте цю таблицю і приклейте всередині судна біля радіостанції, разом з карткою MAYDAY.**
