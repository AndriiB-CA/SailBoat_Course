# Bilingual Sailing Glossary — English ⇄ Українська
# Двомовний словник вітрильних термінів

**🇨🇦 EN** — Organised by topic, because that's how you'll need it. The **"Say it"** column gives the English pronunciation in Cyrillic for words whose spelling misleads — sailing English is full of these, and mispronouncing them is the fastest way to be misunderstood on a boat.

**🇺🇦 UA** — Організовано за темами, бо саме так вам це буде потрібно. Колонка **«Вимова»** дає англійську вимову кирилицею для слів, чиє написання обманює — у вітрильній англійській таких багато, і неправильна вимова — найшвидший спосіб бути незрозумілим на судні.

## 17. The most confusing words in sailing English / Найзаплутаніші слова

**🇨🇦 EN / 🇺🇦 UA** — These trip up every learner. Learn them deliberately.

| Word | The confusion | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **Tack** | Three meanings | (1) the sail's lower forward corner / галсовий кут; (2) which side the wind is on / галс; (3) to turn the bow through the wind / поворот оверштаг |
| **Sheet** | Not a sail, not fabric | A **rope** that controls a sail's angle / шкот |
| **Line vs rope** | Both are "мотузка" | A **line** has a job on the boat; **rope** is the raw material |
| **Luff** | Noun and verb | Noun: the sail's leading edge / передня шкаторина. Verb: to flutter, **or** to turn towards the wind |
| **Draft** | Two meanings | (1) how deep the boat sits / осадка; (2) the depth of curve in a sail / пузо |
| **Head** | Three meanings | (1) the toilet / гальюн; (2) the sail's top corner / топовий кут; (3) "head to wind" / у левентик |
| **Beam** | Two meanings | (1) the boat's width / ширина; (2) 90° to the side ("abeam") / траверз |
| **Leeward** | Spelling vs sound | Written "leeward", said **"LOO-ard"** |
| **Gunwale** | Spelling vs sound | Written "gunwale", said **"GUN-el"** |
| **Boatswain** | Spelling vs sound | Written "boatswain", said **"BOH-sun"** |
| **Forecastle** | Spelling vs sound | Written "forecastle", said **"FOKE-sl"** |
| **Bow** | Homograph | The front of the boat — rhymes with "cow", **not** with "low" |
| **Gybe / jibe** | Two spellings | Same word. British "gybe", American "jibe". |
| **Windward / weather** | Interchangeable | Both mean the side the wind comes from |
| **Port** | Two meanings | (1) left side / лівий борт; (2) a harbour / порт |
| **Knot** | Two meanings | (1) speed unit / вузол швидкості; (2) a tied knot / зав'язаний вузол |
